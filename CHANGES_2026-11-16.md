## 1. Database (migration 039)

Kept: one-delivery wallet reserve (`internal.wallet_reserve`,
`get_wallet_reserve`), the wallet forecast (`internal.wallet_forecast`,
`get_wallet_forecast`), the notice log (`wallet_notice_log`, now `source =
'manual'` plus `claimed_by`), `mark_wallet_notice_result`.

Removed (never live): `wallet_reminder_settings`, `get/set_wallet_reminder_settings`,
`claim_wallet_reminders`, `internal.can_manage_wallet_reminder`,
`claim_low_balance_notices`.

Added:
- `get_low_balance_customers(date)` (`daily_ops:view`): customers whose wallet
  cannot cover their pending, not-held wallet orders on that date — balance,
  required, shortfall, notice status (none / sent / failed / sending).
- `claim_manual_low_balance_notices(date, users)` (`daily_ops:edit`): of the
  chosen customers, claims only those still short and not already notified
  (a failed one may be sent again) and returns them.

Tests (section 13): listing, adding up two orders, exclusions (covered, cash
on delivery, skipped, held, already charged), customer refused, only chosen
and still-short customers sent, no duplicate, sent never resent, failed can be
resent, recharged-since not sent, no reminder objects exist, grants.

## 2. Admin panel

- **Daily Ops → Low wallet balance** (shown when the day has orders):
  customer, phone, wallet, needed, short by, notified. Everyone not yet
  notified is selected; "Already notified" rows are greyed out and cannot be
  selected. **Send to N customers** sends only to the selected ones and shows
  "X sent, Y failed" (plus how many were skipped because they were already
  notified or had recharged).
- Removed: `/api/cron/wallet-reminder`, Settings → Wallet & Notifications and
  its API, `CRON_SECRET` from the README, the reminder hint on the area form,
  and every low-balance push sent by Finalize (including the old
  "balance < 3 × today's debit" one). The "Delivery skipped: low balance" push
  for orders Finalize skips is unchanged.
