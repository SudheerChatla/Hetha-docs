# Order flow fixes: plan

Written 2026-10-15. **Nothing in this document has been implemented yet.** It
is the plan for fixing how one-time orders and subscription deliveries are
processed, recorded, charged and refunded. Pick items up in the order of the
[roadmap](#roadmap) and log each one in the dated `CHANGES_*.md` file when it
is done.

It came from an end-to-end review of:
- the database functions (`docs/db/functions*.sql`, migrations up to 035);
- the admin panel (Daily Ops, Deliveries and Orders screens);
- the customer app (checkout, subscriptions, wallet).

**How sure each finding is:**

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ | Verified directly in the code during the review (the pause/start-date bug was also reproduced with Node) |
| 🔎 | Found by reading the code, consistent with what was checked, but not reproduced |

None of this was run against the live database.

## 1. How it works today

### Two separate pipelines

| | One-time order | Subscription delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Created by | App checkout (`place_order`) or admin "Create Order" | `create_subscription`; each day's row is generated by Daily Ops **Generate** (TypeScript, `Hetha_admin/services/dailyOps/generateRunSheet.ts`) |
| Record per delivery | `orders` + `order_items` | `subscription_daily_orders` + items (one row per subscription per day) |
| When money is taken | **At order time**: wallet debit or Razorpay; COD collected at the door | **Before delivery**, when Daily Ops **Finalize** runs for the day: wallet debit of the *ordered* amount |
| Delivery date | Set automatically: tomorrow, or the day after if past cut-off, snapped to the area's delivery day (trigger `set_order_delivery_date`, migration 033) | The day the run sheet is generated for |
| Marking delivered | Deliveries screen → `record_order_delivery` (033) | Daily Ops status / Reconcile / delivered-qty field |
| Short delivery | ✅ Refunded to the wallet automatically | ✅ **Never refunded**: charged in full |

### One-time orders
1. **Placing:**
   - **Wallet:** the DB prices the order, checks the 3-day buffer and debits the wallet. The order starts as `placed`/`paid`.
   - **COD:** the order starts as `placed`/`pending`.
   - **Razorpay:** the edge function captures the payment first. `finalize_order_payment` (008) then creates the order, already marked paid.
2. **Date:** the DB sets it. 🔎 The app shows a hard-coded "today + 2 days" at checkout (`Hetha_app/lib/screens/checkout/checkout.dart:26-34`) and never reads the real date.
3. **Delivery:** `record_order_delivery` takes delivered quantities per item.
   - Full delivery → `delivered`.
   - Partial → `delivered`, and the shortfall is refunded to the wallet.
   - Zero → `cancelled`; items are refunded, the **delivery charge is not**.
   - ✅ It refunds only if `payment_status = 'paid'` (033:527). Razorpay money goes back to the **wallet**, never to the card.
4. **Orders page dropdown:** ✅ writes any status directly (`Hetha_admin/app/api/admin/orders/[id]/route.ts:16-20`), with no rules on transitions. No delivered time, no quantities, **no refund**.

### Subscriptions
1. **Setup:** item prices are frozen at creation. The frequency comes from the delivery area.
2. **Day edits:** stored as `subscription_daily_orders` rows with `is_customer_modified = true`, billed at the **current** catalog price. Normal days use the frozen price.
3. **Daily Ops:**
   - **Generate** builds the day.
   - **Finalize** (`finalize_daily_run`) locks it and debits `total_value` (the ordered amount) for `status = 'pending'` rows.
   - **Reconcile** marks every remaining row `delivered`.
4. **Recording delivery:** ✅ delivered quantities can be saved, but no SQL ever reads them. No status change, no money.
5. **Refunds:** 🔎 none in code. Skipped, short, or paused/cancelled after charge are all left to manual wallet credits by staff.

### Same address, subscription + one-time order
- 🔎 The Deliveries screen groups by address, so both show as **one stop** only if they point to the **same `addresses` row**. A re-added address, or an old subscription with no address, gives **two stops**, possibly on two routes.
- Daily Ops screens and their printed sheets show **subscriptions only**.
- 🔎 Moving a subscription day (overrides, migrations 020/021) leaves the one-time order on the original day, so the van makes **two trips**.
- 🔎 **Routes live in three places:**
  - `addresses.route_id`, used by Deliveries;
  - `subscriptions.route_id`, used by Daily Ops, its sheets, CSV and overrides;
  - `subscription_daily_orders.custom_route`, free text per day.

  Nothing syncs them, so the two printed sheets can disagree.
- There is no driver entity ("Delivery partners" are courier companies), and nothing is recorded per stop.

## 2. Fixes

Each fix lists: **today** (how it happens), an **example**, the result **after**
the fix, the **changes** to make, the **effort**, and any **decision** needed.

### A. Customers stop being charged for things they didn't get

#### Fix 1: Pause days and start dates handled correctly
**Today (✅).** `generateRunSheet.ts` treats the delivery day as **India
midnight** (`new Date(date + 'T00:00:00+05:30')`, line 149). It reads pause,
start and item-start dates with `new Date('YYYY-MM-DD')`, which is **UTC
midnight**, 5½ hours later (lines 180, 241-243, 254). Results:
- **The first day of every pause is still delivered and charged.** A one-day
  pause never works.
- **A subscription or item is not delivered on its own start date.**

**Example.** Pause from 15 to 20 Oct:

| Day | Should be | Today |
|---|---|---|
| 15 Oct | paused | ❌ delivered and charged |
| 16–20 Oct | paused | ✅ paused |
| 21 Oct | delivered | ✅ delivered |

A subscription starting on 15 Oct is first delivered on 16 Oct.

**After.** Pauses cover exactly the chosen days; subscriptions start on their
start date. The app calendar, the driver's sheet and the wallet charge agree.

**Changes**
- `generateRunSheet.ts`: parse every date (`pause_start_date`,
  `pause_end_date`, `start_date`, `end_date`, `item_start_date`,
  `item_end_date`) as an India-time date, the same way as the target day.
- Same file, cut-off check (lines 228-231): it uses `setHours` in the
  server's time zone (UTC on Vercel). Compute it in India time.
- Unit tests: first pause day, one-day pause, start date, end date, item start
  and end.

**Effort:** ½ day. **Risk:** very low. **Most urgent:** it is charging
customers today.

#### Fix 2: Short and skipped subscription deliveries are refunded
**Today (✅).** Finalize charges the *ordered* amount before delivery. The
delivered-qty field (`deliveriesService.recordSubscriptionDelivery`,
`services/dailyOps/orders.ts updateDeliveredQty`) is never read by any SQL.
Marking a day skipped or cancelled (`bulk-update-status`) moves no money. One-time
orders, by contrast, are refunded by `record_order_delivery`.

**Example.** 2 L milk a day at ₹60/L, so ₹120 is charged at Finalize. The van
delivers 1 L, or the driver skips the customer: they still pay ₹120.

**After.** One "Save delivery" action per subscription day:
1. records delivered quantities;
2. sets the status (delivered / partially delivered / skipped);
3. credits the difference to the wallet if the day was paid, as a ledger
   entry such as "Refund: 1 L milk not delivered on 15 Oct".

The customer sees it instantly in the Wallet tab.

**Changes**
- **New migration**, function `record_subscription_delivery(daily_order_id,
  items, reason)`, modelled on `record_order_delivery` (033):
  - staff-only, locks the row, `delivered_qty <= quantity`;
  - refunds `(ordered − delivered) × unit_price` only if
    `payment_status = 'paid'`, ledger type `subscription_refund`;
  - one refund per day (see Fix 6).
- **Admin:**
  - the Deliveries screen and the Daily Ops delivered-qty field call this
    function instead of writing quantities directly;
  - the Daily Ops skipped/cancelled statuses go through it with
    delivered = 0.
- **App:** label the new ledger type in the wallet transaction list
  (`walletpage.dart`).
- **Tests:** full, short, zero, unpaid/COD, and twice in a row (the second is
  a no-op).

**Effort:** 2–3 days. **Decision:** is "customer not home" refunded? (See
[§3](#3-decisions-needed-before-starting).)

#### Fix 3: Pausing or cancelling after a day was already charged
**Today (🔎).**
- Pausing (`upsert_pauses`) and cancelling (`cancel_subscription`, 030) only
  remove days that are **not yet charged**. After Finalize, a pause or cancel
  for tomorrow removes and refunds nothing.
- Customer-edited days (`is_customer_modified`) survive a later pause or
  cancel and are still charged and delivered.
- Finalize does not re-check the subscription's status, pauses or end date.

**Example.** Thursday: the customer edits Saturday to 3 L. Friday: they pause
Saturday to Monday. Saturday's edited row is still delivered and charged; the
app shows it as paused.

**After.**
- Days not yet charged are removed, including edited days.
- Days already charged are either refused with a clear message ("Tomorrow's
  delivery is already being prepared, so the earliest you can pause is
  Sunday") or refunded.
- What the app shows always matches what is charged.

**Changes**
- **New migration:**
  - `upsert_pauses` and `cancel_subscription` also delete edited, unpaid rows
    inside the range;
  - for paid rows, refuse past the cut-off or refund using Fix 2's helper;
  - `finalize_daily_run` skips rows whose subscription is paused, cancelled or
    past its end date that day (safety net).
- **App:** show the earliest allowed pause or cancel date, based on whether
  tomorrow is already finalized.

**Effort:** 2 days. **Decision:** refuse or refund (see §3).

#### Fix 4: Cancelling a one-time order refunds the customer
**Today (✅).** The Orders page dropdown writes any status directly.
- **Cancelled** on a paid order refunds nothing.
- **Delivered** skips delivered quantities and the delivery time, so short
  deliveries are never refunded.

A manual wallet credit (`update_wallet_balance`) is the only remedy, and it
isn't linked to the order. 🔎 Customers have no way to cancel. 🔎
`payment_status` never becomes `refunded`, and COD never becomes `paid`.

**Example.** ₹450 of ghee paid from the wallet; out of stock; staff set
Cancelled. The wallet stays ₹450 lower.

**After.**
- **Cancel** refunds items and delivery charge to the wallet, records who
  cancelled and why, and sets `payment_status = 'refunded'`.
- **Delivered** goes through `record_order_delivery`.
- Only valid transitions are allowed:
  - placed → processing → out_for_delivery → delivered;
  - cancelled from any open state.

  Delivered or cancelled orders can't be reopened.
- Optional: customers can cancel before the cut-off, with an automatic refund.

**Changes**
- **New migration:**
  - `cancel_order(order_id, reason)`: refund if paid, set
    `cancelled_at`/`cancelled_by`/`cancellation_reason`,
    `payment_status = 'refunded'`;
  - a trigger on `orders` rejecting invalid status transitions;
  - `record_order_delivery` also refunds the delivery charge on zero delivery,
    and sets `payment_status = 'paid'` for COD once delivered.
- **Admin, Orders page:**
  - replace the free dropdown with actions: "Mark out for delivery",
    "Record delivery…" (same form as Deliveries), "Cancel order…" (reason plus
    refund preview);
  - `orders/[id]/route.ts` calls the functions instead of
    `.update({ status })`.
- **App (optional):** a "Cancel order" button on order details before the
  cut-off.

**Effort:** 2–3 days. **Decisions:** wallet or card refund for Razorpay;
customer self-cancel (see §3).

#### Fix 5: Card payment taken, but no order created
**Today (🔎).** Razorpay captures money before the order exists.
- If `finalize_order_payment` → `place_order_core` then fails (an item went
  out of stock or inactive, a scope or address check fails), the money is kept
  with no order and no refund. The app says "contact support"
  (`checkout.dart:208-214`).
- If the app dies after paying, nothing settles (`REMAINING_FIXES.md` item 4).
- `payment_pending` orders and payment intents never expire (item 6).

**After.** Every captured payment ends in exactly **one order** or **one
wallet credit of the same amount**.

**Changes**
- **Edge function `razorpay`:** a `payment.captured` webhook calling the same
  settle function as the app (already idempotent per payment id).
- **New migration:**
  - if order creation fails after capture, credit the captured amount to the
    wallet (ledger type `payment_credit`) and mark the intent instead of
    raising;
  - a scheduled job expiring intents and `payment_pending` orders after 30
    minutes.
- **App:** "₹X has been added to your wallet" instead of "contact support".

**Effort:** 2–3 days. Needs the webhook secret configured in Razorpay.

### B. Money moves exactly once

#### Fix 6: No double refunds and no double charges
**Today (🔎).** No database rule says an order or day is charged or refunded
once.
- **Double refund:** an order is delivered short and refunded. Staff set it
  back to `placed` with the Orders dropdown and record delivery again: refunded
  again.
- **Double charge:**
  1. Finalize charges the day.
  2. A super admin Unlocks it (`runManagement.ts`), which resets `is_finalized`
     and keeps `payment_status = 'paid'`.
  3. Generate deletes the "clean" row (✅ its filter doesn't check
     `payment_status`) and recreates it unpaid.
  4. Re-Finalize charges again.

  `wallet_transactions` has only a non-unique index on the reference.

**After.** The ledger enforces "once". A duplicate charge or refund is
rejected by the database, whatever screen or route triggers it.

**Changes**
- **New migration:**
  - a unique rule on `wallet_transactions(reference_type, reference_id)` for
    charge and refund types;
  - `apply_wallet_delta` raises on a duplicate;
  - check live data for existing duplicates first, and review them manually.
- **Admin:**
  - `generateRunSheet.ts` never deletes a row that is paid, delivered or has a
    ledger entry;
  - Unlock keeps paid rows locked, or post-unlock edits charge or refund only
    the difference.
- Fix 4's transition trigger blocks reopening orders.

**Effort:** 1–2 days. Protects against future bugs of the same kind.

#### Fix 7: Failed payments are not silently delivered for free
**Today.**
- A Finalize charge that fails (for example a low balance) sets
  `payment_status = 'failed'`.
- ✅ Reconcile (`runManagement.ts`) marks every remaining pending row
  `delivered`, including failed ones. The charge is never retried and nobody is
  told.
- 🔎 Finalize notifications (`daily-ops/route.ts:175-192`) tell every customer
  "wallet debited", including failed and COD ones.

**After.**
- Failed payments are listed before the van leaves, with retry / deliver as
  COD / skip.
- Reconcile is blocked while any remain unresolved.
- Notifications say what actually happened.

**Changes**
- **Admin, Daily Ops:** a "Failed payments" panel; "Retry charge" calls a new
  `retry_daily_order_charge(id)`.
- **Admin, Reconcile:** skip or block failed rows.
- **Admin, notifications:** separate messages for charged, failed and COD.

**Effort:** 1–2 days.

### C. The app's rules hold

#### Fix 8: Subscription rules cannot be bypassed
**Today.**
- ✅ "Revert cancellation" (`subscription_page.dart:803-820`) updates the row
  directly. `guard_subscription_update` (009) only blocks `user_id` and
  `payment_method`, so customers can reactivate any cancelled subscription,
  skipping the 5-subscription limit and the 3-day buffer, or edit
  area/frequency/dates.
- 🔎 `pending_cancellation` never becomes `cancelled`:
  `SubscriptionStatusManager.processScheduledCancellations` has no callers, and
  the cron job touches only `active` and uses UTC dates. These subscriptions
  inflate `get_user_daily_commitment` and the 5-subscription count forever.
- 🔎 Customers can insert pauses directly (RLS); `pause_service.dart` falls back
  to direct writes.
- 🔎 Day-edit cut-offs are client-only, and `modify_daily_order_core` (025)
  compares with UTC `CURRENT_DATE` instead of `today_ist()`.

**After.** Every rule is enforced in the database. The app only shows friendly
messages.

**Changes**
- **New migration:**
  - `revert_cancellation(subscription_id)` checking the limit, the buffer and
    the end date;
  - remove customers' direct UPDATE/INSERT on `subscriptions` and
    `subscription_pauses` (all changes through functions);
  - a nightly job (India time) completing ended `pending_cancellation`
    subscriptions, plus a fix for the existing expiry job's dates;
  - day-edit functions check the area cut-off and use `today_ist()`.
- **App:** `subscription_page.dart` calls `revert_cancellation`; remove the
  direct-write fallback in `pause_service.dart`.

**Effort:** 2 days.

### D. One clear picture for operations

#### Fix 9: One route per address, one delivery sheet
**Today (🔎).** Routes live in three places with no sync (see
[§1](#same-address-subscription--one-time-order)). There are two printed sheets:
- Daily Ops: subscriptions only, a row per subscription, with signature
  columns;
- Deliveries: merged, a card per address, but no route headings and no load
  summary.

When a customer moves address to another area, migration 033 clears the
address route but not the subscription route.

**Example.** A customer moves from Sector 15 (route A) to Sector 20 (route
B). Daily Ops prints them on route A, while Deliveries shows them unassigned.
Two drivers each think the other has it, or both go.

**After.**
- `addresses.route_id` is the only route.
- One printed sheet per route, with:
  - a load summary ("Milk 1 L × 42…");
  - stops in order, with subscription and one-time items together;
  - signature and COD-collected columns.
- Daily Ops keeps only Generate, Finalize, failed payments and Reconcile.

**Changes**
- **New migration:**
  - backfill addresses without a route from their subscription;
  - retire `subscriptions.route_id` (or expose it read-only from the address);
  - replace the `custom_route` text with a proper optional per-day route
    override read by both screens.
- **Admin:**
  - Daily Ops sheets, CSV and overrides read the address route;
  - the Subscriptions page stops editing routes;
  - Deliveries print gets route headings, a load summary, stop order and
    signature columns.

**Effort:** 3–4 days. The biggest operational change.

#### Fix 10: Same-address deliveries arrive together
**Today (🔎).**
- Stops merge only on the same `addresses` row.
- One-time order dates ignore the subscription and overrides.
- An alternate-day area without `reference_date` gives one-time orders **no
  date** (they never appear on Deliveries), while the generator delivers the
  subscription every day.
- The checkout date is hard-coded.

**After.**
- A one-time order for an address with an active subscription is delivered on
  the subscription's next delivery day ("comes with your milk"), and checkout
  shows that real date.
- Moving a subscription day moves its same-address orders (or warns).
- Duplicate addresses are reported and can be merged.

**Changes**
- **New migration:**
  - the order-date trigger uses the customer's subscription at that address;
  - overrides also move same-address one-time orders;
  - a constraint: `delivary_frequency = 2` requires `reference_date`;
  - attach a saved address to old subscriptions that have none, from their
    snapshot.
- **Admin:** a "possible duplicate addresses" report with merge.
- **App, checkout:** show `expected_delivery_date` from the DB.

**Effort:** 2–3 days.

## 5. Overall effect

**Customers**
- Pauses work from the first day; subscriptions start on their start date.
- Short or skipped deliveries and cancelled orders are refunded automatically
  and show at once in the Wallet tab.
- A card payment always ends in an order or a wallet credit.
- The delivery date shown is the real one; one-time orders come with their
  milk.

**Operations**
- One route per address and one printed sheet with a load summary.
- One way to record each delivery; one place for failed payments.
- No manual wallet credits to fix mistakes.

**Business and accounts**
- Every ledger entry is tied to exactly one order or day and cannot repeat.
- `payment_status` tells the truth: paid, refunded, COD collected.
- Rules (subscription limit, wallet buffer, cut-offs) are enforced by the
  database, so a modified app or a staff slip can't break them.
- Fewer support calls: "I paused but was charged", "I wasn't home but paid",
  "my payment went through but there's no order".
